About Us

W3 Digital Development creates and supports digital initiatives that strengthen communities, improve access to information, advance digital literacy, and deliver lasting public benefit.


We believe technology should be accessible, useful, and available to organizations and individuals regardless of financial resources.

Our Mission

To empower communities through technology, education, and digital innovation by creating digital resources, providing technical support, advancing digital literacy, and developing projects that deliver lasting public benefit.

Board & Governance

W3 Digital Development Inc. is governed by a board of directors committed to responsible leadership, transparency, and the organization’s community-focused mission.

The board provides oversight of the organization’s activities, finances, policies, and community initiatives. Revenue and resources are used to support the organization’s nonprofit purposes and public-benefit projects.

Governance Principles

  • Community benefit and responsible stewardship
  • Transparency and accountability
  • Ethical and inclusive decision-making
  • Responsible management of organizational resources
  • Oversight of projects, including NL Explorer
