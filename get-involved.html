Get Involved

Help W3 Digital Development build useful digital resources, strengthen communities, and improve access to local information.

Make a Community Contribution

W3 Digital Development Inc. welcomes contributions from individuals, community organizations, nonprofit groups, photographers, outdoor enthusiasts, and technology professionals.

You do not need advanced technical skills to contribute. Local knowledge, suggestions, corrections, photographs, videos, and community connections can all help our projects grow and remain useful.

Ways to Get Involved

Suggest a Location

Recommend a trail, park, scenic viewpoint, heritage location, attraction, or community destination that could be considered for NL Explorer.

Submit a Correction

Let us know when trail information, directions, facilities, access conditions, or destination details need to be corrected or updated.

Contribute Photos or Videos

Contribute original photographs or videos that help visitors understand a location, trail, attraction, or community experience.

Share Local Knowledge

Provide historical details, accessibility information, community knowledge, safety observations, or practical visiting information.

Volunteer Technical Skills

Help with website development, testing, accessibility, mapping, research, writing, data review, digital media, or other technical work.

Become a Community Partner

Community organizations, nonprofit groups, educators, tourism organizations, and public-interest initiatives can contact W3DD about possible collaboration.

Contribution Guidelines

To help us review a contribution, please provide as much relevant information as possible. Depending on the type of contribution, this may include:

  • The name and location of the trail or destination;
  • A description of the information being suggested or corrected;
  • The date the information, photograph, or video was collected;
  • Directions, accessibility details, or other useful visitor information;
  • A source for historical or factual information, when available; and
  • Confirmation that you created or have permission to share any submitted photographs or videos.

Please do not submit confidential information, sensitive personal information, copyrighted material that you do not have permission to share, or content that could place people, private property, protected sites, or wildlife at risk.

Photograph and Video Contributions

Only submit photographs or videos that you created or have permission to provide.

Before submitted media is published, W3 Digital Development may request written permission describing how the media may be displayed, credited, edited, or removed.

Submitting material does not guarantee publication. Contributions may be reviewed for accuracy, relevance, quality, safety, privacy, accessibility, and consistency with the organization’s community-focused mission.

Volunteer Opportunities

Volunteer opportunities may depend on current W3DD projects, available supervision, and organizational needs. Possible activities may include:

Content Research

Research trails, destinations, community resources, and publicly available historical information.

Website Testing

Test pages, links, maps, videos, navigation, and mobile layouts, and report problems.

Accessibility Review

Help identify ways to make W3DD projects clearer and more accessible to a wider range of visitors.

Contact Us

To volunteer, suggest a location, submit a correction, discuss a community partnership, or ask about making a contribution, contact W3 Digital Development.
